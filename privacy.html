Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026

Summary: ComplianceCalc does not collect, store, or process any personal data through its compliance tools. All tool calculations run entirely in your browser. We use Google AdSense to serve advertisements and may use Google Analytics for anonymous usage statistics.

1. Who we are

ComplianceCalc ("we", "us", "our") operates the website at YOUR-DOMAIN.com (the "Site"). We provide free online compliance tools for UK employment law and UAE business setup. The Site is operated as an independent information service.

If you have questions about this privacy policy, you can contact us at: privacy@YOUR-DOMAIN.com

2. What data we collect

No personal data is collected through our compliance tools. All inputs you enter into our tools — contract details, working hours, business activities, and similar information — are processed entirely within your web browser. This data is never transmitted to our servers, never stored, and never shared with any third party by us.

We do not operate any user accounts, registration forms, or sign-in systems. We do not set any first-party cookies for tracking or authentication purposes.

The only data that may be collected about your visit to this Site is:

  • Standard server access logs, which may include your IP address, the page requested, the date and time of the request, and your browser type. These logs are used for security and performance monitoring only and are not used for profiling or targeted advertising by us.
  • Anonymous usage data collected by Google Analytics, if you have not opted out (see Section 5).

3. Cookies

ComplianceCalc does not set any first-party cookies. We do not use cookies for tracking, user identification, or preferences.

Third-party services that operate on this Site — specifically Google AdSense and Google Analytics — may set their own cookies on your device. These cookies are governed by Google's privacy policy and cookie policy, not ours. See Sections 4 and 5 for details.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling third-party cookies will prevent Google AdSense from serving personalised advertisements but will not affect your ability to use any of our compliance tools.

4. Google AdSense

This Site uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google AdSense is operated by Google LLC, a company incorporated in Delaware, USA, with European operations managed by Google Ireland Limited.

When you visit pages on this Site, Google AdSense may set cookies on your device and collect certain information about your browsing behaviour across websites to serve advertisements that are relevant to your interests. This is known as interest-based advertising or personalised advertising.

The data collected by Google AdSense for advertising purposes is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

You can opt out of personalised advertising by Google in the following ways:

  • Visit Google Ads Settings to manage your Google ad preferences
  • Use the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page
  • Use the Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out tool
  • Install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on

If you opt out of personalised advertising, you will still see advertisements on the Site, but they will not be tailored to your interests.

5. Analytics

This Site may use Google Analytics to collect anonymous information about how visitors use the Site. Google Analytics collects information such as how often users visit, which pages they visit, and what other sites they used prior to visiting. We use this information solely to improve the Site and its tools.

Google Analytics collects only the IP address assigned to you on the date of your visit, not your name or other identifying information. We do not combine the information collected through the use of Google Analytics with personally identifiable information.

Google's ability to use and share information collected by Google Analytics about your visits to this Site is restricted by the Google Analytics Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy.

You can prevent Google Analytics from recognising you on return visits by disabling cookies on your browser or by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

6. Third-party links

Our tools and content pages may contain links to external websites such as ACAS, GOV.UK, HMRC, DIFC, and other regulatory and government bodies. These links are provided for informational convenience only.

When you follow a link to an external website, that website's own privacy policy will apply to any data you provide or any data collected about your visit. ComplianceCalc has no control over, and accepts no responsibility for, the content or privacy practices of third-party websites.

7. Children's privacy

This Site is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal data from children under 13. Our compliance tools are professional tools intended for adults working in business and HR contexts. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child, please contact us at privacy@YOUR-DOMAIN.com and we will take steps to delete that information.

8. Your rights

Because we do not collect personal data through our tools, the standard data subject rights under UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018 — including the right of access, rectification, erasure, and portability — are generally not applicable to information you enter into our tools (since we do not hold it).

For data collected by Google through AdSense or Analytics, you should direct any requests or complaints to Google directly via Google's Privacy Policy or your Google account settings.

If you have any concerns about how data is handled on this Site, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, or for other operational reasons. When we update the policy, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the Site following any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or our data practices, please contact us:

  • Email: privacy@YOUR-DOMAIN.com
  • Website: YOUR-DOMAIN.com

We will aim to respond to all privacy enquiries within 30 days.
