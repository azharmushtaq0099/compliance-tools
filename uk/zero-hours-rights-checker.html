What is the ERA 2024 Guaranteed Hours Right?

The Employment Rights Act 2024 introduces a landmark new right for workers on zero-hours and variable-hours contracts. After working regular, predictable hours for 12 consecutive weeks, workers will have the right to request a contract that reflects their actual working pattern.

This is a significant step forward for the estimated 1 million zero-hours workers in the UK, providing greater financial certainty and employment security without fully removing the flexibility that some workers prefer.

Key provisions

  • The 12-week qualifying period must be continuous — a gap of more than one week resets the count
  • Both zero-hours and variable-hours contract workers are covered
  • Agency workers are explicitly included in the right
  • Employers must offer guaranteed hours reflecting the worker's regular pattern, or provide written reasons within a statutory timeframe
  • Dismissal or detriment for making a guaranteed hours request will be automatically unfair
  • Workers can bring employment tribunal claims for unreasonable refusal or detriment

Implementation timeline

The ERA 2024 received Royal Assent in October 2024. The guaranteed hours provisions are expected to come into force in 2026 via secondary legislation. Workers should begin keeping detailed records of their working patterns now so they are prepared to make a formal request once the law is in force.

Frequently Asked Questions

When do zero-hours workers get the right to request guaranteed hours?

Under ERA 2024, zero-hours workers who work regular and predictable hours for 12 consecutive weeks gain the right to request a guaranteed-hours contract. The employer must offer guaranteed hours reflecting the worker’s regular pattern or provide legitimate business reasons for not doing so. This provision is expected to come into force in 2026 via secondary legislation.

Can an employer refuse to offer guaranteed hours?

Yes, but only in limited circumstances. The employer must provide a written statement of reasons within a specific timeframe. Refusing without legitimate reasons or dismissing a worker for requesting guaranteed hours will be automatically unfair. Workers can bring an employment tribunal claim if their request is unreasonably refused.

Does the ERA 2024 zero-hours right apply to agency workers?

Yes. The Employment Rights Act 2024 extends the guaranteed hours right to agency workers working through employment agencies. Both the agency and the end client (hirer) may have obligations. The 12-week qualifying period applies in the same way.

What counts as ‘regular hours’ for the 12-week test?

The definition of ‘regular’ hours for the ERA 2024 guaranteed hours right will be set out in secondary legislation. Generally, working similar hours on a predictable pattern over a sustained period is expected to qualify. Sporadic or highly variable hours may not meet the threshold. The government has indicated a broad definition to cover typical zero-hours working patterns.

What should zero-hours workers do now?

Zero-hours workers should keep a record of the hours they work each week, noting start and end times and total hours. Once the guaranteed hours provisions come into force (expected 2026), workers who have 12+ consecutive weeks of regular hours will be able to make a formal request. Workers should also check whether their employer has updated their flexible working policy to allow day-one requests, which is already in force.
