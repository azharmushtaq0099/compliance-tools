What ComplianceCalc does

ComplianceCalc provides free compliance tools for two professional audiences: HR and legal teams in the UK managing employment law obligations, and entrepreneurs and advisors navigating UAE company formation.

Our UK tools cover the Employment Rights Act 2024 — the most significant piece of UK employment legislation in 25 years — plus supporting rights around zero-hours contracts and statutory holiday entitlement. Our UAE tools cover free zone comparison across 12 major jurisdictions and full setup cost modelling.

All tools run entirely in your browser. We do not collect, store, or transmit any of the information you enter. There is no account required and no data is saved on our servers.

Who these tools are for

  • HR managers and HR Business Partners who need to audit employment contracts and policies for ERA 2024 compliance
  • Employment lawyers and HR consultants using the tools as a structured first-pass assessment with clients
  • Business owners and directors managing their own HR compliance without a dedicated HR team
  • Entrepreneurs and startup founders researching UAE company formation options and comparing free zone costs
  • Corporate services providers who advise clients on UAE business setup and need quick, structured cost comparisons

How we ensure accuracy

Every tool is built directly from primary legislative and regulatory sources — we do not rely on secondary summaries or third-party interpretations as our source of truth.

  • UK tools: Built from the Employment Rights Act 2024 as enacted, the Employment Rights Act 1996 as amended, the Working Time Regulations 1998, ACAS guidance, and EHRC statutory codes of practice
  • UAE tools: Built from free zone authority official fee schedules, UAE Federal Law No. 2 of 2015 (Commercial Companies Law), and published MOHRE regulations
  • Monthly review: We review each tool against official source material every month and update it when guidance or legislation changes
  • ERA 2024 tracking: We monitor secondary legislation commencement orders as they are laid in Parliament, updating effective dates and provisions as they come into force

Our tools are intended to reflect the law as it stands at the date shown in the footer. They cannot account for individual circumstances, case law, or employer-specific factors.

Who built this

ComplianceCalc was built by professionals with experience in HR compliance and legal technology. The tools are designed to meet the standards expected by HR practitioners and legal advisors — not to be a simple checklist.

We built ComplianceCalc because professional compliance tools are routinely locked behind expensive SaaS subscriptions or consultant fees, while the underlying statutory requirements are public information. Our belief is that accurate, well-designed compliance tools should be freely accessible to every business regardless of size.
