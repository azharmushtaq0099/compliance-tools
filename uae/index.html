Which UAE Free Zone is Best for Your Business?

The UAE has over 40 free zones, but most businesses should focus on the 10–15 largest by reputation and infrastructure. The right choice depends on your business activity, budget, visa needs, and whether clients expect a Dubai address. Here are the top recommendations by use case:

Cheapest setup
Ajman Free Zone / Fujairah Free Zone
Licence fees from AED 5,750/year. Ideal for consultants, freelancers, and businesses that don't need a physical Dubai presence. No minimum capital requirement.
Best Dubai value
IFZA (International Free Zone Authority)
Dubai-based licence from AED 12,900/year with no minimum capital. Flexible activity combinations, up to 6 visa allocations, and a strong reputation with banks.
Trading & crypto
DMCC (Dubai Multi Commodities Centre)
World's #1 free zone (Global Free Zones of the Year 2024). Required for precious metals, diamond trading, and crypto asset businesses. Licence from AED 20,000/year.
Financial services
DIFC (Dubai) / ADGM (Abu Dhabi)
Common law jurisdictions with English courts. Required for fund management, investment advisory, and regulated financial services. Higher setup cost but unmatched regulatory credibility.
Technology
Dubai Internet City / Dubai Silicon Oasis
Purpose-built tech clusters with access to talent and a strong ecosystem. Dubai Internet City hosts Google, Microsoft, and Meta regional offices. Good for tech startups seeking investor credibility.
Freelancers
Fujairah Creative City / UAQ Free Trade Zone
Freelancer permits from AED 7,500/year. Covers media, creative, consulting, and IT activities. Ideal for solo operators who want UAE residency without a full company licence.
