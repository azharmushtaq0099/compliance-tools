Understanding UAE Free Zone Costs

Setting up a UAE free zone company involves several cost categories that first-time founders frequently underestimate. Here is what to expect across each major line item.

What's included in this calculator

  • Trade license fee — the core annual license cost, varies by free zone and activity type
  • Establishment card — required for processing employee visas, AED 1,500–2,000/year
  • Residence visa costs — each visa covers medical, Emirates ID and residency stamping (AED 4,500–6,500 per person)
  • Office or desk fee — flexi-desk from AED 2,000/year; private offices from AED 8,000/year
  • Registration and admin fees — company registration, MOA and share certificates
  • PRO services — document processing and government liaison (AED 2,000 estimated)

What's not included

  • Bank account opening — typically no fee but requires a minimum balance of AED 25,000–100,000 at most UAE banks
  • Annual audit fees — required for some free zones (AED 3,000–8,000/year)
  • Accounting and bookkeeping — AED 3,000–6,000/year for a small company
  • Health insurance — mandatory for all UAE visa holders (AED 1,500–5,000/person/year)
  • Corporate tax registration — required if annual revenue exceeds AED 375,000 (9% UAE CT applies)
  • Formation agent fees — if using a third-party agent (AED 1,500–5,000 typically)

Annual renewal costs

Beyond the first-year setup, plan for annual renewal costs of typically 60–80% of initial setup. Visa renewals fall due every 2–3 years at approximately AED 3,000–5,000 per visa. DMCC charges an annual subscription fee in addition to the trade license. DIFC and ADGM have additional regulatory fees tied to their financial services licensing framework.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

  • What is included in UAE free zone setup costs?

    UAE free zone setup costs typically include: the trade license fee, company registration and admin fees, an establishment card, an office or flexi-desk fee, residence visa costs per person (covering medical, Emirates ID and residency stamping), and PRO/document processing. First-year totals range from around AED 15,000 for a budget free zone with one visa to AED 200,000+ for premium free zones with multiple visas.

  • How accurate are these cost estimates?

    The estimates are based on published 2026 free zone tariffs and typical market rates. They are indicative only — actual costs can vary by 10–30% depending on activity type, current promotions, agent fees and whether bundled packages are available. Always request a formal quotation from the free zone authority or a registered formation agent before making a final decision.

  • What additional costs should I budget for?

    Beyond core setup costs, budget for: bank account minimum balance (AED 25,000–100,000 depending on the bank), annual audit fees where required (AED 3,000–8,000), accounting and bookkeeping (AED 3,000–6,000/year), health insurance for each visa holder (AED 1,500–5,000/person/year), and corporate tax registration if annual revenue exceeds AED 375,000. Formation agent fees of AED 1,500–5,000 apply if you use a third-party service.

  • What is a UAE establishment card?

    An establishment card (also called an e-channel establishment card or company establishment card) is required to process employee residence visas in the UAE. It is issued by MOHRE (Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation) and costs approximately AED 1,500–2,000. It must be renewed annually and is mandatory for any UAE company that intends to sponsor employee or investor visas.

  • Which UAE free zone is the cheapest?

    Fujairah Creative City is currently the cheapest option with license fees from AED 5,750. Ajman Free Zone starts from AED 6,000. RAKEZ in Ras Al Khaimah starts from AED 7,500. For Dubai specifically, IFZA is the most cost-effective starting from AED 12,900. Budget free zones are based outside Dubai but are fully legitimate and MOHRE-registered for all standard business activities.
